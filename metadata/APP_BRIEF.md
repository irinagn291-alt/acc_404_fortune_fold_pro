# Fortune Fold

## What it is

Fortune Fold is a paper-folding puzzle game for players who enjoy spatial puzzles and improving their solutions. Fold illustrated paper edges to bring marks together, release a paper tiger, and bridge gaps on its way home. Each completed sheet earns a print in the “Album”, with a gold impression for finishing at or under par.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On a fresh launch, the desk shows “Fortune Fold”, the chapter “Letters”, a red envelope with a raised corner and gold seal, and the first sheet title, “Whiskers”.
2. The instruction reads “Pull the raised corner to open.” Drag the raised corner down and left or tap “Open the sheet”. The corner also has the accessibility label “Pull the raised corner to open.” and can be activated directly with VoiceOver. “Album” is available before opening the envelope.
3. The first sheet opens with “Whiskers”, “Letters”, “Sheet 1”, “Folds 0”, and “Par 2”. The clue reads “Slide the cut edges to meet the missing whiskers and ear.” Fold “Whisker edge” right and “Ear edge” left, either by dragging or by selecting an edge and using the corresponding arrow.
4. Once the marks meet, the status reads “The marks meet. Press the seal to release the tiger.” Tap “Release tiger”. The tiger walks to the gate and the sheet shows “Print made”, “Gold impression earned”, and “Best 2” for this two-fold solution. Tap “Next sheet” to continue.
5. After a sheet has been opened, a cold launch returns to the desk with the saved chapter and sheet title. The envelope instruction still reads “Pull the raised corner to open.” The button reads “Continue folding” and reopens the saved sheet.

## Screens

### Desk

The visible heading is “Fortune Fold”. The desk shows the current chapter, the red envelope, and the current sheet title.

- “Open the sheet” opens the first sheet. After the first opening, “Continue folding” reopens the active sheet in its saved state.
- The raised corner opens the same sheet when pulled far enough down and left.
- “Album” opens the collection with the active sheet and its chapter selected.

### Puzzle sheet

The heading is the sheet’s own title. The header shows the chapter, a campaign-wide sheet number such as “Sheet 1”, the current fold count such as “Folds 0”, and the target such as “Par 2”. The board shows foldable paper pieces, marks, the tiger, its trail, and the destination. The status line gives a sheet clue or feedback about the current move.

- The door icon, labelled “Desk” for accessibility, returns to the envelope screen. The book icon, labelled “Album” for accessibility, opens the collection at the active sheet.
- Tap an exposed paper edge to select it, or drag it along its hinge to fold it directly. The edge names in the table below are accessibility labels. An edge reports “Folded left”, “Flat”, or “Folded right” as its accessibility value.
- The arrow icons are labelled “Fold left” and “Fold right” for accessibility. Each moves the selected edge one position in that direction, including back to flat. With no selection, the accessibility hint is “Select an edge”. With a selection, it gives the edge’s name.
- “Undo” restores the preceding state, including the fold count, paper positions, and tiger progress. A release can also be undone.
- “Hint” can select and highlight a hinge and show “Try the highlighted hinge.” It can also show “Try the gold seal while these marks are showing.” or “Undo a move to free a hinge.”
- “Release tiger” is the seal control. It brightens when all required marks are visible. A successful release starts the tiger along the trail without adding a fold. Once released, the tiger proceeds automatically whenever a fold opens the way.

### Completed sheet

The board and header stay in place. The play controls give way to “Print made” or, on “Homeward”, “The tiger is home”. The result reads “Gold impression earned” at or below par, or “Try again to match the par.” above par. The best recorded fold count appears as text such as “Best 2”.

- “Undo” steps back into the puzzle while preserving the print already earned.
- “Replay” resets the current sheet to its starting layout and zero folds while keeping its recorded best.
- “Next sheet” starts the following sheet. On “Homeward”, that action is “Album” and opens the collection.
- The header’s “Desk” and “Album” controls remain available.

### Album

The heading is “Album”. The print counter starts at “Prints 0 / 16” and can reach “Prints 16 / 16”. A scrollable two-column grid contains four illustrated sheet cards for the selected chapter. Wider layouts also show a larger preview beside or above the selected sheet’s details.

- “Desk” returns to the envelope screen, including when the “Album” was opened from a puzzle.
- “Letters”, “Lanterns”, “Rooftops”, and “Palace” switch the grid to that chapter and select its first sheet. All four chapter controls are available from the start.
- Tapping a card selects it and shows its title, status, and available action. The exact card titles are the sheet titles in the table. Unfinished cards display “Available” or “Locked”. Completed cards show a best count such as “Best 3” or “Gold · best 2”, alongside a preview of the best recorded print.
- “Open the sheet” opens an available sheet. It resumes the current unfinished sheet if that sheet has never earned a print. Selecting a different available sheet starts a fresh attempt.
- “Replay” starts a fresh attempt on a completed sheet.
- Selecting a locked card shows the preceding sheet required to open it, for example “Finish Whiskers to open this sheet.”

## Features

- 16 “Sheet” puzzles across “Letters”, “Lanterns”, “Rooftops”, and “Palace”.
- Drag-to-fold paper hinges or select an edge and use “Fold left” and “Fold right”.
- Match marks through folds, overlapping pieces, and cut-out windows. Uncover tucked edges, turn a fold through flat, bridge gaps, and open a shortcut.
- “Release tiger” sends the animated paper tiger along the trail. It waits at breaks and moves on as the route opens.
- “Undo” and contextual “Hint” during play.
- “Folds” and “Par” tracking, “Gold impression earned” for a finish at or below par, and “Best” fold counts.
- “Album” with sequential sheet unlocks, print previews, and a count of distinct completed sheets.
- “Replay” and “Continue folding” for new attempts and saved progress.
- Paper, seal, pawstep, and bridge sounds, with haptic feedback on supported devices. The app follows the system’s Reduce Motion setting for folds and tiger movement.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- **The envelope snaps back.** A short pull or a pull in the wrong direction leaves it closed. Pull farther down and left, or tap “Open the sheet” or “Continue folding”, whichever is shown.
- **An arrow is dim or an edge won’t move.** Select an exposed edge to enable a valid “Fold left” or “Fold right” move. Move the covering piece first if an edge is tucked underneath. A mark can be visible through a window while the underlying edge is still inaccessible. Go through “Flat” to move from one folded side to the other. A short or misaligned drag can spring back without adding to “Folds”.
- **The seal rejects a tap.** “The seal is still open. Bring the missing marks together.” means required marks are missing. Fold until “The marks meet. Press the seal to release the tiger.” appears, then tap “Release tiger”.
- **The tiger stops after release.** “The tiger is waiting at a break in the path.” means the next gap still needs a bridge or an open shortcut. The second sheet instead says “The tiger stopped at the brook. Look at its tail.” in UK English or “The tiger stopped at the creek. Look at its tail.” in US English. Fold “Tail flap” to bridge that gap. “Release tiger” stays disabled after release because the tiger resumes on its own. If the last move was the release, “Undo” can take it back.
- **Controls pause while the tiger moves.** While “The tiger is on its way.” or “The tiger tests the bridge.” is shown, folding, “Hint”, “Undo”, and “Release tiger” are disabled. Wait for the tiger to stop or reach the gate. “Desk” and “Album” can leave the sheet during the animation, and reopening shows the tiger at the position it reached for that move.
- **“Undo” starts dim.** Make a fold or successfully release the tiger to create a step to undo. When “Hint” says “Undo a move to free a hinge.”, step back and try another fold.
- **The “Album” starts at “Prints 0 / 16”.** It already shows previews, but only “Whiskers” is “Available” initially. A “Locked” card can be selected for its requirement. Complete the preceding sheet and it becomes “Available”, even if the preceding print was above par.
- **A replay doesn’t add another print.** The counter tracks distinct completed sheets. A lower fold count replaces the saved best and its preview. “Replay” resets the attempt, while “Undo” after a finish preserves the earned print and unlocked progress.
- **Navigation returns to the envelope.** “Desk” in the “Album” always returns to the desk. Tap “Continue folding” to return to the active sheet. The final result’s “Album” button opens the collection instead of starting another sheet.
- **A save fails.** The app can display “The desk could not save this move.” as an inline status message. It has no separate alert, retry button, or settings screen. A subsequent move, undo, or reopening the sheet attempts to save again.

## Starter content and resume

The full 16-sheet campaign is included. A fresh start has the “Whiskers” envelope ready to open, zero earned prints, and no previous attempt. Some puzzles have pieces already folded or tucked beneath another piece as part of their starting layout.

The app saves one active attempt, including its fold count, folds, tiger position, release state, and undo history, as well as the best prints for completed sheets. “Continue folding” resumes the active attempt after relaunch. Starting another sheet or choosing “Replay” replaces the active attempt. In the “Album”, “Replay” starts over even if the active sheet is a partly played replay. Use “Continue folding” from the desk to resume that replay.

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent.

Gameplay progress and print records stay on this device. The campaign works offline. There is no on-screen “Contact” or “Support” control.

## Platform

- iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.0 or iPadOS 17.0. iPhone is portrait only. iPad supports portrait, upside-down portrait, and both landscape orientations.
- The game has the same campaign and rules in every region. It follows the system’s language and locale, with UK English as the default localization. Supported languages are UK English, US English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic. Numbers in counters are locale-formatted. Arabic uses right-to-left layout around the board while the paper, envelope gesture, and fold arrow directions stay physically consistent.
- The app name shown in English is “Fortune Fold”. It is “叠纸迎福” in Simplified Chinese, “摺紙迎福” in Traditional Chinese, and “طيّ الحظ” in Arabic. The English wording above follows UK English except where a US English variant is specified.
