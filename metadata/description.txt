Fold cut-paper panels to reveal the marks that form a tiger. Once every required mark is visible, press the seal and watch the tiger follow an ink trail towards the gate.

Work through 16 puzzle sheets across four chapters. Look through cut-out windows, uncover tracks on the reverse side and move paper bridges between gaps. If the tiger stops at a gap, a fold that opens the route will send it on its way again. Use Undo to take back a move or ask for a hint when you get stuck.

Earn a gold impression by finishing a sheet at or below its target fold count. See your collected prints and best fold counts in the Album, replay unlocked sheets and return to your saved attempt from the Desk.

Fortune Fold plays offline on iPhone and iPad.
