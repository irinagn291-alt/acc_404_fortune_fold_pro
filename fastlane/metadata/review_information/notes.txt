On a fresh launch, tap Open the sheet or drag the raised envelope corner down and left. Follow the first sheet’s clue. Drag a reachable panel or select it and tap a fold arrow. Press Release tiger once all required marks are visible. Reaching the gate completes the sheet and unlocks the next one. Open the Album to check the saved print and best fold count. Return to the Desk and choose Continue folding to resume the current attempt. All sheets are played through the regular campaign.

The game works offline, saves progress in the app’s local storage and requests no runtime permissions. It has no accounts, backend, cloud sync, analytics, ads, purchases, Game Center or third-party services. AVFoundation plays bundled sounds on physical devices, where selected actions also provide haptic feedback. Sound and haptics are disabled in the simulator.
