Fold cut-paper panels to reveal the marks that make up a tiger. When all the required marks are visible, press the seal to send the tiger along an ink trail to the gate.

Solve 16 puzzle sheets across four chapters. Peek through cutout windows, find tracks on the back of the paper, and move paper bridges between gaps. If the tiger stops at a gap, fold a panel to open the route and the tiger will pick up where it left off. Use Undo to take back a move or ask for a hint if you’re stuck.

Match or beat a sheet’s target fold count to earn a gold impression. The Album keeps your collected prints and best fold counts. Replay an unlocked sheet from the Album or continue your saved attempt from the Desk.

Fortune Fold works offline on iPhone and iPad.
